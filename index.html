    About this model

    What it is
    A 34-second interactive visualization of one illustrative plasmonic biosensor: PDMS channel, gold split-ring array, localized gap mode, surface capture, and an ensemble transmission readout.
    Who it is for
    Someone learning the transduction chain, from polarized light to a redshifted transmission minimum.
    What it does not claim
    It is not a fabricated device, an electromagnetic simulation, a kinetics fit, or a clinical assay. Numbers other than the 20 nm gap are illustrative design parameters.
    How the picture is structured
    One master clock drives the camera, cover, field, molecules, charts, and sound. Seeking recomputes state from time. It does not depend on frames already played.
    How to use it
    Play, pause, replay, or scrub. Keys: Space, arrows, Home, End, 1–5, M, R, F. Audio starts only after you play. Music and effects have separate levels.
    Scientific choices
    The mode is an electric-dipole gap resonance for this geometry and polarization, not a universal split-ring response. Binding is modeled as an increase in local refractive index that shifts an existing dip to longer wavelength, with depth and linewidth held constant. Occupancy is near-field-weighted. The optical cycle and the binding sequence are both slowed relative to their physical timescales, and those timescales are not the same.
    Discovery, conversion, money
    This page is the explainer. It does not collect leads, set analytics cookies, or sell anything.
    Launch and revision
    Ship as a static build. Replace the schematic spectrum only when a documented simulation or measurement exists, and label that source.
